// About

Researcher.
Developer. Engineer.

I'm a PhD student at the Lassonde School of Engineering, York University, working under the supervision of Prof. Hany E. Z. Farag. My research sits at the intersection of transportation systems, spatial analytics, and energy infrastructure.

My work focuses on rethinking how we plan EV fast-charging networks — moving beyond traditional approaches to develop meso-level, mobility-aware methods that better capture real-world charging demand patterns across urban landscapes.

Beyond research, I build full-stack tools — from charging emulators using ISO 15118 protocols to interactive dashboards for spatial optimization — always grounded in rigorous analysis and clean engineering.
